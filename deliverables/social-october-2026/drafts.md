## 2026 Symposium | Keynote: Christopher Procyshyn

What will Canada's next chapter in biopharmaceuticals look like, and where will you fit in?

Join @Christopher Procyshyn at the BioHubNet 2026 Annual Symposium for a keynote connecting the future of Canada's biopharmaceutical sector with the people who will help shape it.

His session: "Biopharmaceuticals and Canada: Where we are, When we need to go, and How You Are Going To Take Us There."

As former CEO of Vanrx Pharmasystems and a member of Canada's federal Vaccine Task Force, Chris brings a perspective spanning manufacturing, innovation and national capacity.

Make it a day of fresh perspectives and new connections, with discussions on AI, career opportunities and biomanufacturing, plus networking across the life sciences community.

October 29, 2026 | Chelsea Hotel, Toronto
