## Negative keyword changes

Keep the BioHubNet brand exclusions requested by the user. Do not treat more exclusions as automatically better: use narrow phrase/exact terms and check actual search queries. Google negative matching does not expand to synonyms or singular/plural forms; the current documentation says casing and misspellings are handled automatically. [Google negative keyword rules](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en)

### Review existing broad exclusions

These are proposed revisions for the editable dashboard. Preserve the recorded live baseline until changes are applied and verified.

Never use these as blanket negatives: **free, funded, funding, grant, grants, student, science, biology, STEM, medicine, master's, PhD, postdoc, jobs, internship, internships, career, training, course, courses, online, travel, startup, startup founder, investor, or eligible university names.** Do not globally block country names: trainees can seek international investor meetings or placements while living inside the pilot area.

## Audience and campaign structure recommendation

These audience problems are inferred from the program design and the user's brief, not claimed to be measured research.

| Intent | Audience/problem | Message | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ENGAGE | Eligible graduate students need industry skills without paying for another full credential | Institution + graduate role + funded training | ENGAGE / training-credit application |
| EXPERIENCE | Eligible master's/PhD trainees and postdocs need industry experience and help presenting research skills to employers | Institution + internships + profile support | EXPERIENCE / talent-pool application |
| VentureConnect | Trainee founders need travel/event funds to meet investors and commercial partners | Founder identity + meeting/travel problem + up to $5,000 | EQUIP / VentureConnect |

Prepare the three program intent groups within the CAD $600/month plan: ENGAGE, EXPERIENCE and VentureConnect. Build institution variants within the relevant intent groups. Avoid a separate paid campaign for every university at this budget. A separate founder campaign can accommodate distinct dates, intent and conversion goals; reallocate within the existing total budget. The team confirms VentureConnect is opening soon; prepare all its materials now.
